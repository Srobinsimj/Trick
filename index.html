🚩 Phishing Red Flags — Educator Notes

  1. Fake URL: Domain is studentlogin.net, not daystar.ac.ke. The padlock is real but irrelevant — it only means the connection is encrypted to the attacker's server.
  2. Urgency/pressure: A real phishing email would say "Your account will be suspended in 24 hours — sign in now."
  3. Credential harvesting: Anything typed in the fields below is captured and would be sent to the attacker. Nothing is validated.
  4. Social engineering: The page mimics real branding (logo, colors, language) to lower suspicion.
  5. No 2FA prompt: After "sign in," victims are redirected to the real site — they may never notice.
